Delete your Stego Bun account

Stego Bun is made by Lance N.

In the game (quickest): open Stego Bun, tap the gear at the top right, then Delete account. Confirm, and your account is deleted from our server straight away.

By email: if you can't open the game, email hello@stegobun.com from the address of the Google account linked to your Stego Bun (or tell us your in-game details), with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Delete your game but keep your account: in the game, tap the gear, then Start over. This erases Stego Bun and everything in your game from our server (finds, decorations, companions, the pebbles he found, postcards and progress) and starts a new egg; your account (and its Google link, if any) stays, and so do any pebbles you bought: the new egg starts with them. The record of past game actions is kept until you delete your account.

What's deleted when you delete your account: your account, Stego Bun and everything in your game (finds, decorations, companions, pebbles, postcards and progress), and the record of your game actions.

What's kept: nothing from your game, and our record of your purchases goes too. Google Play (which handles payments) and RevenueCat (which checks purchases for us) keep their own purchase records, as the law and their own policies require.
